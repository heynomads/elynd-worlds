Right now, you are safe. Let's slow everything down together.
Breathe in for four... hold gently... and breathe out for six.
Picture a quiet harbour at sunset. A small wooden rowboat is tied to an old post, rocking gently on the water.
With every breath out, the boat settles a little more. The water is calm, the sky soft pink and gold.
Breathe in for four... and out for six. Your breath is the tide, slow and steady.
