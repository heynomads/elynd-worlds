# technique=body_scan mood=tense language=en minutes=10
Bring your attention to your feet. Notice their weight, their warmth.
Now imagine you are lying in a summer meadow at midday, the grass soft beneath you, a wooden fence at the far edge of the field.
Let your attention move up through your calves and knees, releasing a little tension with every breath out.
A white butterfly rests on a tall stem of grass nearby, its wings opening and closing slowly.
Move up through your hips, your belly, your chest. Breathe in, and feel your ribs widen. Breathe out, and soften.
