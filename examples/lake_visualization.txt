# technique=visualization mood=stressed language=en minutes=10
Let your shoulders drop as you settle in. Breathe in slowly through your nose... and let it go.
Imagine you are walking down a narrow path through tall pines, early in the morning. The air is cool and smells of resin.
The path opens onto a small lake. The water is perfectly still, and a thin mist hangs just above it.
At the edge of the water there is a smooth grey stone, warm where the first light touches it. You sit down beside it.
A pine branch reaches out over the water above you, its needles still wet with dew.
Breathe in... and feel the cool air fill your chest. Breathe out... and let the stillness of the water settle inside you.
Nothing here needs anything from you. The lake simply holds the sky.
