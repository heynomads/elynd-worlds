# technique=sleep_journey mood=tired language=en minutes=20
It is late now, and the day is done. Let your body grow heavy against the bed.
Picture a small wooden cabin in the hills. Outside the window, the last blue light is fading behind the ridge.
On the windowsill, a single candle lantern glows softly, its light gold and steady.
Each breath out is a little slower than the one before. There is nowhere to be.
The night is quiet and kind. Let the lantern's glow grow softer... and softer... as you drift towards sleep.
