We built two things during the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (26 Oct – 4 Nov 2026): ELYND Worlds and ELYND Journeys. ELYND itself (its writer, voices, music and player) existed before.

1ELYND Worlds

Every meditation opens on a living painted world.

In the ELYND app, each guided meditation's world is a calm painted place to arrive in and come back to, alive as such a place would be: one from a library Meta's models painted, or a new one painted on demand for a very personal meditation.

Visit elynd.app Code on GitHub

Sound on: a one-minute ELYND meditation in one of the app's own voices, with its music; the worlds on the screen change with it, as in the app. Or see , without sound.

The minute's voice, words and music are ELYND's own, from before the hackathon, played from elynd.app.

A personal world · 2 Nov 2026

The courtyard

An excerpt of what we typed into the app (our own test words, not a listener's):

  • my grandmother's courtyard
  • her red watering can
  • my old dog Bruno

Llama 3.3 reads the meditation and turns it into a place, with no names and nothing about the listener, checked in code: from the meditation, only that description (with its technique, length and language) reaches Meta's API.

Turned down · 4/10

A real rejection

Muse Spark Open said no

Muse Spark's design, for the world library: A bright flower meadow runs to a high cliff edge above a calm blue sea. Muse Spark Open looked at the painting:

4/10. “Both the smooth stone and wooden post are visible in bright midday light, but the meadow is rendered as a floating, disconnected strip above the sea with a mirrored grass band below, breaking the continuous cliff-edge and creating a chaotic, unsettling spatial effect.”
  • on brief: no
  • calm: no
  • stray figures: none
  • text: none

Its fix for another try: “Change the design to require a single continuous ground plane where the meadow runs solidly to the cliff edge above the sea with no floating, cut-out, or mirrored layers.”

Muse Spark had passed it with 8/10 while the open judge was busy. Every painting passed that way went back to Muse Spark Open, which has the final say: it passed 73 of 74 and turned this one down. No listener saw it. In the app only Muse Spark Open may pass a painting; when none passes, the listener keeps a calm world from the library.

What you are seeing

Alive as such a place would be

Each world lives as such a place would: the water ripples and its reflections wobble, now and then the sun glints on it; the clouds drift; the grass waves in the wind; a stream flows; a lantern flickers; leaves, snow, pollen or fireflies drift through the air.

The objects move as such things move: a boat sinks a little on the swell and rolls, a butterfly floats and folds its wings, branches sway, stones and posts stay still.

Play as a meditation

A minute of ELYND, in one of the app's own voices with its music, played from ELYND's website (its voice, words and music existed before the hackathon). The worlds change with it as in the app: whole for 45 seconds while the listener arrives, dimmer after, whole again at the end.

A sample ten-minute meditation, 12× faster and without sound, shows the rest: the world fades to a glow while the eyes are closed, more of it returns in a long silence, all of it at the end, when time has passed there too (a morning sun has climbed, a golden light has deepened). A sleep world stays dim and warm, and ends dark.

2ELYND Journeys

Thirty days of meditations toward one goal.

A world is where one meditation happens; a journey is thirty days of meditations toward one goal. Llama plans them, and you walk them as one climb up a painted mountain, a step a day.

Planned by Llama, walked a step a day.

A Llama 3.3 guide listens, then plans thirty days; ELYND writes each day's meditation around one small “when …, I …” step for a situation you named, and the day before an event you name, the meditation rehearses it.

Scroll to walk the thirty days ↓

How a journey is made, in order

  1. Llama 3.3open weightsthe guide listens and sums up what it heard
  2. Llama 3.3plans the thirty days
  3. Muse Imagepainted the climb once: six paintings up one mountain
  4. Muse Spark Open or Muse Sparkjudged each painting

A real plan · made on production, 2 Nov 2026

“Speaking up in meetings”

“To speak up in meetings with your thoughts, even if imperfect.” Ready about ten seconds after the guide's summary was confirmed; the answers were our own test words, not a listener's.

  1. Week 1 · Noticing momentsWhen do you stay silent in meetings?
  2. Week 2 · Gentle kindnessHow do you support yourself in challenging discussions?
  3. Week 3 · Practising expressionImagine sharing your thoughts with your manager present.
  4. Week 4 · Embodying confidenceFeel your body when you speak up in a meeting.

Day 1, “Starting to speak up”: You begin to notice your thoughts in meetings.

  • Each day's meditation is written and voiced by ELYND's own writer and voices, which existed before the hackathon and are not Meta models.
  • The guide and the planner run on Llama 3.3 on Amazon Bedrock, in ELYND's own AWS account, so a listener's answers never reach Meta's Model API.
  • The climb is painted once and is the same for every listener; the walk steps into it with depth maps from Depth Anything V2 Small, made once offline (not a Meta model).
Week 1 · days 1–7

ELYND Journeys · the climb

The climb: one place, six paintings

A journey is thirty days toward one goal: Llama 3.3 plans each day, and ELYND writes the day's meditation. You walk it as one climb up one mountain, a painting a week. Each after the first is a Muse Image edit of the painting before it, further along the same path, so it stays one place (one between weeks 3 and 4 is not shown; the last looks back from the top); a critic passed every one.

Week 1 · days 1–7: the first painting of a journey's world, by Muse Image · Muse Spark Open 10/10

Each day steps a little into the painting, by depth maps from Depth Anything V2 Small (Yang et al., 2024, arXiv:2406.09414; Apache-2.0), made once offline: not a Meta model. The climb is painted once and is the same for everyone; nothing of a listener goes into it.
