ELYND WORLDS

Every meditation opens on a living painted world.

ELYND writes and voices a new guided meditation for each listener. ELYND Worlds turns each meditation into a calm place you can see: an AI agent designs it, paints it, checks it, cuts out what moves and finds the water, the sky and the grass, so the painting lives as such a place would. These are worlds the agent made for the example meditations in this repository.

  • Muse Spark · designs, checks, revises
  • Muse Image · paints and repaints
  • SAM 3.1 · cuts the layers, finds the water, sky and grass, checks the plate

What you are seeing

Each world lives as such a place would: the water ripples and its reflections wobble, now and then the sun glints on it; the clouds drift; the grass waves in the wind; a stream flows; a lantern flickers; leaves, snow, pollen or fireflies drift through the air. The objects move as such things move: a boat sinks a little on the swell and rolls, a butterfly floats and folds its wings, branches sway, stones and posts stay still. Play as a meditation runs a ten-minute session sped up: the whole world while the listener arrives, a glow while their eyes are closed, more of it in a long silence, all of it at the end, when time has passed in the place too: a morning world's sun has climbed, a golden one's glow has deepened. A sleep world stays dim, ends dark, and its light stays warm. This page draws it all with the phone's own shader (WebGL 2), and the same timeline: the Python, Dart and JavaScript versions are tested against each other frame by frame.
