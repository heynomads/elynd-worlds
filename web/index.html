ELYND Worlds

Every meditation opens on a living painted world.

In the ELYND app, each guided meditation's world is a calm painted place to arrive in and come back to, alive as such a place would be: one from a library Meta's models painted, or a new one painted live for a very personal meditation.

ELYND Journeys

Thirty days toward one goal, planned by Llama.

A guide on Llama 3.3 listens, then plans thirty days: each day's meditation offers one small when-then step (“when …, I …”) for a situation you named, and the day before a moment you name, the meditation rehearses it. The journey's world climbs one mountain, a step a day.

  3. Muse Imagepaints the climb: six paintings up one mountain
  4. Muse Spark Open and Muse Sparkjudged each painting

A real plan · made on production, 2 Nov 2026

“Speaking up in meetings”

“To speak up in meetings with your thoughts, even if imperfect.” Ready about ten seconds after the guide's summary was confirmed; the answers were our own test words, not a listener's.

  1. Week 1 · Noticing momentsWhen do you stay silent in meetings?
  2. Week 2 · Gentle kindnessHow do you support yourself in challenging discussions?
  3. Week 3 · Practising expressionImagine sharing your thoughts with your manager present.
  4. Week 4 · Embodying confidenceFeel your body when you speak up in a meeting.

Day 1, “Starting to speak up”: You begin to notice your thoughts in meetings.

  • Each day's meditation is written and voiced by ELYND's own writer and voices, which existed before the hackathon and are not Meta models.
  • The guide and the planner run on Llama 3.3 on Amazon Bedrock, in ELYND's own AWS account, so a listener's answers never reach Meta's Model API.
  • The climb is painted once and is the same for every listener; the walk steps into it with depth maps from Depth Anything V2 Small, made once offline (not a Meta model).

Week 1 · days 1–7

A journey · thirty days

The climb: one place, six paintings

A journey is thirty days toward one goal: Llama 3.3 plans each day, and ELYND writes the day's meditation. Its world is one walk up one mountain, a painting a week. Each after the first is a Muse Image edit of the painting before it, further along the same path, so it stays one place (one between weeks 3 and 4 is not shown; the last looks back from the top); a critic passed every one.

Week 1 · days 1–7: the first painting of a journey's world, by Muse Image · Muse Spark Open 10/10

Each day steps a little into the painting, by depth maps from Depth Anything V2 Small (Yang et al., 2024, arXiv:2406.09414; Apache-2.0), made once offline: not a Meta model. The climb is painted once and is the same for everyone; nothing of a listener goes into it.

Turned down · 4/10

A real rejection

The judge said no

Muse Spark's design, for the world library: A bright flower meadow runs to a high cliff edge above a calm blue sea. Muse Spark Open looked at the painting:

4/10. “Both the smooth stone and wooden post are visible in bright midday light, but the meadow is rendered as a floating, disconnected strip above the sea with a mirrored grass band below, breaking the continuous cliff-edge and creating a chaotic, unsettling spatial effect.”
  • on brief: no
  • calm: no
  • stray figures: none
  • text: none

Its fix for another try: “Change the design to require a single continuous ground plane where the meadow runs solidly to the cliff edge above the sea with no floating, cut-out, or mirrored layers.”

Muse Spark had passed it with 8/10 while the open judge was busy. Every painting passed that way went back to Muse Spark Open, which has the final say: it passed 73 of 74 and turned this one down. No listener saw it. In the app only Muse Spark Open may pass a painting; when none passes, the listener keeps a calm world from the library.

The example worlds

Made by the agent for the example meditations in this repository, each alive as on the phone. Open the X-ray on any of them.

What you are seeing

Alive as such a place would be

Each world lives as such a place would: the water ripples and its reflections wobble, now and then the sun glints on it; the clouds drift; the grass waves in the wind; a stream flows; a lantern flickers; leaves, snow, pollen or fireflies drift through the air.

The objects move as such things move: a boat sinks a little on the swell and rolls, a butterfly floats and folds its wings, branches sway, stones and posts stay still.

Play as a meditation

A ten-minute session, sped up: the whole world while the listener arrives, a glow while their eyes are closed, more of it in a long silence, all of it at the end, when time has passed in the place too: a morning world's sun has climbed, a golden one's glow has deepened.

A sleep world stays dim, ends dark, and its light stays warm.

The phone's own shader

This page draws it all with the phone's own shader (WebGL 2), and the same timeline: the Python, Dart and JavaScript versions are tested against each other frame by frame.
